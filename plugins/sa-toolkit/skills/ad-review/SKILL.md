Ensure architectural decision based on good architectureal patterns

1. Check alternatives are compliant with the target architecture specified on confluence page https://wiki.tools.nykredit.it/spaces/EA/pages/231017655/Target+Architecture and subpages
2. Check alternatives are focused on clean architecture, maintainability, security and separation between services and user interface
3. Check business rules, including validation and calculation is placed in the services
4. Display comments and suggest improvement of pro and con
5. Check if architectural decision is written in clear and easy understandable english
